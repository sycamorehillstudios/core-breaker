Privacy Policy — Core Breaker

Last updated: May 31, 2026 · Developer: Sycamore Hill Studios

This Privacy Policy explains how Core Breaker (the “Game”), published by Sycamore Hill Studios (“we”, “us”), handles information when you play. We built Core Breaker to respect your privacy: we do not require an account, and we do not collect or sell personal information ourselves.

1. Information we collect

  • We collect no personal information directly. There is no sign-up, login, email, or name required to play.
  • Game progress (your planets, currencies, upgrades and settings) is stored locally on your device only. It is not uploaded to us.

2. Information collected by third parties

The Game may use the following Google services. When enabled, these third parties may collect data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising). May access a device advertising identifier and limited device/usage information to serve and measure ads. We do not receive your personal data from this. See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information. Rewarded ads are optional and opt-in.
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases). Payments are processed by Google; we receive only a purchase confirmation, never your payment details.
  • Google Play services for app delivery, integrity and crash/stability where applicable.

If a feature above is not present in the version you have installed, no data is collected for it.

3. How information is used

  • To save and restore your game progress on your device.
  • To show ads (including optional rewarded ads) and process any purchases you choose to make.
  • To send local notifications (e.g., “your drones are full”). These are generated on your device and are not sent to us. You can disable them in the Game’s Settings or your device settings.

4. Children

Core Breaker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

5. Your choices

  • Reset / delete your data: use “Wipe Save” in Settings, clear the app’s data in Android settings, or uninstall the Game — this removes all locally stored progress.
  • Ads personalization: manage your advertising ID and ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Where shown, you may set your ad-consent preferences via the in-app consent prompt.

6. Data security & retention

Game data lives on your device and persists until you delete it or uninstall the Game. We do not operate servers that store your personal data.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at sycamorehillstudios@outlook.com.
