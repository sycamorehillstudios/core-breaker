Privacy Policy — Core Breaker

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Developer: Sycamore Hill Studios

This page explains what data Core Breaker (the “Game”), made by Sycamore Hill Studios (“we”, “us”), handles and why.

1. No accounts or servers of our own

  • There is no sign-up or login. We don't ask for your name or email.
  • Your game progress (planets, currencies, upgrades and settings) is saved on your device. We don't run a server that receives your data.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The Game shows full-screen ads (interstitials). It also offers rewarded ads, which you can choose to watch for a reward. The Remove Forced Ads purchase turns off the interstitials; rewarded ads stay optional.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob collects and shares data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location, worked out from your IP address
  • how you interact with ads and the Game
  • crash and diagnostic data

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

In regions where the law requires consent, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, the Game shows Google's consent form before it requests ads, so you can choose whether ads are personalized. The Game has no menu to change that choice later. To reset it, clear the app's storage in Android settings or reinstall the Game (this also deletes your progress).

3. Purchases and Core+ VIP (Google Play)

Purchases, including the Core+ VIP subscription, are handled by Google Play. We receive a record that a purchase was made so the Game can deliver it. We never see your payment details. You can cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

4. Reminders

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules reminders on your device. They are created on the device and are not sent to us. You can turn them off in the Game's Settings or in your device settings.

5. Your choices

  • Delete your progress: use “Wipe Save” in Settings, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the Game.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it, or turn off ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

6. What we don't do

We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. We don't use an analytics service of our own, and we don't sell personal data.

7. Children

Core Breaker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at sycamorehillstudios@outlook.com.
